## Hi there, welcome on my GitHub

I'm Simon, a computational microbiologist and bioinformatician currently based in Lausanne, Switzerland working in the group of Prof. Pascale Vonaesch. I hold a PhD in Life Science from the University of Lausanne. I analyse metagenomic, 16S and whole-genome sequencing data from large paediatric cohorts, mainly on gut microbiome and children undernutrition.

## What I work with
- **Languages and tools:** R, Bash (advanced); Python, Snakemake, C++ (basic)
- **Methods:** shotgun metagenomics, strain tracking, AMR gene profiling, 16S amplicon analysis, differential abundance, regression modelling
- **Environment:** HPC clusters, conda, Git/GitHub

## Selected work
- [Satellite_project](https://github.com/yersim/Satellite_project): code for our *Gut Microbes* (2026) paper on strain-level translocation of oral bacteria in stunted children
- [Pastobiome](https://github.com/yersim/Pastobiome): gut microbiota of agropastoral children in Ethiopia (*Sci Rep*, 2023)
- Lab pipeline: [shotgun metagenomics workflow](https://github.com/VonaeschLabUNIL/LAB_BIOINFO_SCRIPTS/tree/main/IN_HUMANS/METAGENOMIC) (VonaeschLabUNIL) - built and maintained reproducible workflows on HPC cluster
